Community Guidelines 📖

How we keep Aapas safe, respectful, and genuinely useful for everyone

🚫 Absolute Rule: No money exchanges hands on Aapas — ever. This is a mutual aid platform, not a marketplace. Any attempt to charge for services will result in immediate removal.

💰 The No-Money Rule

  • Aapas is built on generosity, not commerce. Skills and resources are shared freely.
  • You may not request or offer cash, bank transfers, digital payments, or any form of monetary compensation.
  • Barter (swapping skills for skills) is fine and encouraged.
  • Accepting tips or gifts is at your own discretion, but never expected or advertised.

🤝 How to Have a Good Exchange

  • Be clear in your listing. Specific offers and requests get better responses.
  • Respond to messages promptly — community members are counting on you.
  • Agree on time, place, and scope before meeting. Put it in writing (chat message) to avoid misunderstanding.
  • Mark the exchange as complete once both parties are satisfied.
  • Leave an honest thumbs up (or don't) — feedback helps the whole community.

🛡️ Safety Tips

  • For your first exchange with someone new, meet in a public place.
  • For home visits, let a friend or family member know where you're going.
  • Trust your instincts. If something feels wrong, it's okay to cancel.
  • Never share sensitive personal information (ID number, bank details) with other members.
  • Report any safety concerns immediately using the Report an Issue page.

🌟 Community Values

  • Mutual Aid: We help each other because community thrives when everyone contributes.
  • Respect: Treat every member with dignity, regardless of background, skill level, or need.
  • Reliability: Your word matters. If you commit, follow through.
  • Inclusion: Aapas is for everyone in the community, regardless of age, gender, or ability.
  • Generosity of spirit: The more you give, the stronger our network becomes.

⚖️ Dispute Process

  • Try to resolve disagreements directly and calmly with the other member first.
  • If that fails, submit a report using the Report an Issue page. Include as much detail as possible.
  • Admins review all reports within 48 hours. All reports are private.
  • Possible outcomes: warning issued, listing removed, badge revoked, or member suspended.
  • Admins' decisions are final. We aim to be fair and hear all sides.

🏅 Trust Badges

  • 5 Exchanges — Automatically awarded after completing 5 exchanges.
  • 10 Exchanges — Automatically awarded after completing 10 exchanges.
  • Active Member — Awarded when you have both an active offer and request posted.
  • Verified Neighbour — Manually assigned by admins after identity verification.
  • Community Helper — Manually assigned to exceptional contributors.
